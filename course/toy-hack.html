作品要求 / Project Brief

選一個實體玩具，利用它原本的按法、動作、造型或機構改造一個部位，讓玩具和網頁連動。設計一個可重複操作的回合，讓觀眾看懂自己的動作造成了什麼變化。

Choose a physical toy. Adapt one part by building on how it is pressed, moved, shaped, or assembled. Connect it to a web interface and design one repeatable interaction whose feedback visitors can understand.

玩具當操作介面 / Toy as input

轉動玩具方向盤，網頁上的車跟著轉向，到目的地時顯示「已到站」。

Turn a toy steering wheel to steer a car on screen. The interface shows “Arrived” when the car reaches its destination.

玩具當輸出 / Toy as output

在網頁上選擇表情，玩具眼部的矩陣顯示對應表情。

Select an expression on the web interface. A matrix fitted into the toy’s eye area displays the matching expression.

完成其中一種即可。 雙向連動可依作品需要加入。MAX7219 是期末選用元件，也可使用感測器、按鍵、LED、伺服馬達等元件。

Choose either role. Add two-way interaction if it suits your project. The MAX7219 is optional for the final. You may also use sensors, buttons, LEDs, servos, or other suitable components.

完成一次同儕試玩與一項修改。可以用 AI 協助寫程式、建模或排查問題。發表時請用自己的話解釋一段互動規則。

Run one peer playtest and revise one aspect based on what you observe. You may use AI for coding, modeling, or troubleshooting. At the final, explain one interaction rule in your own words.

參考填法 / Example

Observation: The tester clicked the mouth in the toy photo. The button did not change, so they clicked three more times. The physical mouth moved later.

修改：送出動作後，畫面先顯示「指令已送出」。依程式預定動作時間暫停重複觸發，時間到後顯示「可再操作」。

再次測試：觀察試玩者是否會等待，以及實體是否正常動作，分別記錄。

Retest: Record whether the tester waits and whether the physical toy moves correctly as separate observations.

自選挑戰可以從哪裡開始？ / Where Could Your Challenge Begin?

  • 感測判斷。 分辨觀眾靠近、停留或退回，記錄一次正確判斷與一次誤判，再修改規則。

  • 虛實對應。 畫面顯示實體回傳的狀態，讓觀眾看懂裝置正在回應、已完成或需要再試一次。
    Physical–digital feedback. Show states reported by the device so visitors can recognize responding, completed, or retry states.
  • 玩具改造。 利用玩具原有的機構，完成一個可重複操作的動作，並能讓下一位觀眾重新開始。
    Toy modification. Adapt an existing mechanism to make one action repeatable and reset it for the next visitor.

讓感測器看懂一個行為 / Recognizing an Action with Sensors

選一種想辨認的行為，使用現有的超聲波感測器，依需要加領其他感測器。設計判斷規則，讓網頁模擬器呈現數值、判斷過程與結果，再思考玩具如何回應這項資訊。

Choose an action you want to recognize. Use your ultrasonic sensor and request additional sensors as needed. Design rules and visualize the readings, decision process, and result in your web simulator. Then decide how your toy could respond.

共同暖身｜進入還是離開？ / Warm-up · Entering or Leaving?

在單人依序通過的狹窄通道前後設置 A、B 兩個偵測位置。先用網頁按鈕模擬訊號，再測試實體感測器。A → B 可作為進入的候選順序，B → A 可作為離開的候選順序；請決定何時才算完成通過，以及何時可以開始判斷下一次。

Place detection points A and B along a narrow passage used by one person at a time. Simulate signals with web buttons before testing physical sensors. A → B suggests entry and B → A suggests exit. Define when a passage is complete and when the system is ready for the next person.

  • 正常進入與離開：完成後各計數一次。
    Normal entry and exit: count each completed passage once.
  • 觸發 A 後退回：判斷要等多久，以及如何取消。
    A is triggered, then the person retreats: decide how long to wait and how to cancel.
  • 停在感測範圍：避免同一次停留被反覆計數。
    Someone stays within range: prevent repeated counting of the same visit.
  • 兩處幾乎同時觸發：顯示待判定，說明還需要什麼訊號。
    Both points trigger almost together: show an uncertain state and identify what further evidence is needed.

畫出「等待 → 第一個訊號 → 等待後續訊號 → 確認或取消 → 回到等待」。兩人並排、緊貼或反向同時通過時，記錄系統的判斷限制。

Sketch “Ready → First signal → Await further signals → Confirm or cancel → Ready.” Document the limits when people pass side by side, close together, or in opposite directions at the same time.

你的資訊可信嗎？ / Can You Trust Your Interpretation?

  1. 定義行為。 寫出想辨認的事，例如靠近後觸碰、停留一段時間或通過入口。
    Define the action. State what you want to recognize, such as approaching and touching, staying for a while, or passing through an entrance.
  2. 選擇訊號。 說明需要哪些感測器與擺放位置，以及距離、順序、時間或次數如何支持判斷。
    Choose the evidence. Explain your sensors and placement, and how distance, sequence, timing, or frequency supports the interpretation.
  3. 現場核對。 請同學照預定情境操作，把實際行為與系統判斷並排記錄，保留一次正確判斷與一次誤判或無法判定。
    Check against observation. Have a peer perform your scenarios. Record the actual action alongside the system’s interpretation, including one correct result and one error or uncertain result.
  4. 修改規則。 調整門檻、等待時間、感測器位置或狀態流程，再次測試並說明仍可能判錯的情況。
    Revise the rules. Adjust thresholds, timing, placement, or states. Retest and explain which situations may still cause errors.

例如，距離變短支持「物體靠近」的判斷；要判斷「有人進入」，還需要設定通道、方向與完成通過的條件。

For example, decreasing distance supports “an object is approaching.” Interpreting it as “a person has entered” requires a defined passage, direction, and completion conditions.

模擬器要呈現什麼？ / What Should Your Simulator Show?

原始數值 / Readings

距離、開關狀態或其他感測數值。

Distance, switch states, or other sensor readings.

判斷過程 / Decision Process

目前狀態、觸發順序、等待時間與事件紀錄。

Current state, signal sequence, waiting time, and event history.

結果與回應 / Result and Response

辨認出的行為或待判定狀態，以及玩具預計如何反應。

The recognized action or uncertain state, and the toy’s intended response.

接回期末玩具 / Connect It to Your Final Toy

回答「我的玩具需要知道觀眾的什麼行為？取得這項資訊後，它如何回應？」例如觀眾靠近後觸碰，玩具才醒來；觀眾停留一段時間，玩具逐漸放鬆。以這次練習的規則與視覺化，發展期末互動原型。

Answer “What visitor action does my toy need to recognize, and how should it respond?” A toy might wake only after someone approaches and touches it, or gradually relax when someone stays nearby. Build your final prototype from these rules and visualizations.

課堂完成項目：行為與感測配置草圖、可操作的網頁模擬器、一則測試與修正紀錄，以及玩具回應的構想。

Class checkpoint: an action and sensor-placement sketch, a working web simulator, a test-and-revision note, and an idea for the toy’s response.

抱玩偶觸發音樂 / Hug a Toy to Make Music

玩具當輸入。 Makey Makey 和 Scratch 的工作坊介紹如何用摸、按或抱等日常動作觸發電腦上的聲音。以玩偶為例，擁抱可以成為程式接收的觸發事件，Scratch 再用這個事件播放音樂。

Toy as input. The Makey Makey and Scratch workshop explores how everyday actions such as touching, pressing, or hugging can trigger sound. A hug can become the input that starts music in Scratch.

觀察重點。 擁抱這個動作怎麼被偵測？如果觀眾持續抱住，聲音要播放一次還是持續播放？

Watch for: How could a hug be detected? If the visitor holds the hug, should the sound play once or continue?

會唱歌的湯瑪士 / KaraokeBot

玩具當輸出。 Hunter Irving 取下二手玩具的臉，保留能讓嘴巴與眼睛動起來的機構，再加裝伺服馬達。Arduino 讀取音樂軟體安排好的 MIDI 訊息，依時序開合嘴巴，配上合成歌聲。

Toy as output. Hunter Irving reused a secondhand toy’s face and its moving mechanism. An Arduino reads MIDI messages arranged in music software and drives a servo to move the mouth in time with synthesized vocals.

觀察重點。 找出一個能表達狀態的部位，例如嘴巴開合。試著把案例的 MIDI 訊息換成網頁事件，想像觀眾的哪個操作會觸發這個動作。

Watch for: Identify one moving part that communicates a state. Imagine replacing the MIDI cue with a web event. What would the visitor do to trigger the motion?

網站事件啟動玩具 / Web-Triggered Toy

玩具當輸出。 Roth Mobot 讓網站上的造訪事件啟動一輛改造過的電子警車。網站更新計數，電腦上的程式偵測變更並通知 Arduino，Arduino 再透過光耦合觸發警車原有的燈光與語音。

Toy as output. Roth Mobot made a modified electronic police truck respond to a website visit. A script detects a change in the site’s counter and signals an Arduino, which uses optical coupling to trigger the truck’s existing lights and voice.

觀察重點。 把網站事件和玩具的回應畫成三格流程。指出事件在哪裡發生、誰接收它，以及觀眾能看到或聽到什麼。

Look for: Sketch a three-step flow. Show where the event happens, what receives it, and what the visitor can see or hear.
